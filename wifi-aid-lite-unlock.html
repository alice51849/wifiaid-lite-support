Questions

What can I do without paying?

Your first complete check, with any tool, needs no purchase. That includes the one-tap check of Wi-Fi, DNS and the wider internet, a single-website check, or Deep Check.

What does the one-time unlock add?

Unlimited checks with every tool, full check history, and the before/after comparison after a fix.

Is it a subscription?

No. It is a one-time in-app purchase. There is no subscription.

Privacy stays the same

No account, ads, analytics or tracking. Location is used only to identify the Wi-Fi and node, and is processed on this device. Check history is stored only on this device and is never uploaded.
