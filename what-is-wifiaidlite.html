What is WiFi Aid Lite?

WiFi Aid Lite is an iPhone network checker for when the internet is down or crawling. One tap checks Wi-Fi, DNS and the wider internet in order, then shows which step is failing, the most likely cause and what to try next. You can also check a single website to tell whether the problem is that site or your whole connection, and Deep Check collects more samples to catch drops that come and go. Every result shows its evidence: DNS, TCP, TLS, response time, IPv4 and IPv6.

Who is WiFi Aid Lite for?

People whose Wi-Fi says connected but nothing loads; travellers on hotel, café and airport Wi-Fi that is still waiting for a browser sign-in; anyone whose connection looks fine but feels slow, since repeated samples reveal jitter; and anyone who would rather see where the problem actually is than reboot the router at random.

How does WiFi Aid Lite charge?

Free download with an optional in-app purchase. Your first complete check, with any tool, needs no purchase. A one-time in-app purchase then unlocks unlimited checks with every tool, full history and the before/after comparison after a fix. There is no subscription. For the current price, check the App Store page, because prices differ by country. See what the one-time unlock adds.

How is WiFi Aid Lite different from other network tools?

Based on what the listing describes: checks run in order (Wi-Fi, DNS, internet) and point to the failing step and its likely cause; every result shows its evidence; it spots Wi-Fi sign-in pages; it suggests practical next steps such as opening the sign-in page, trying a trusted DNS service or moving closer to the router; and check history stays on the device and can be compared over time. There is no account, no ads, no analytics and no tracking; location is used only to identify the Wi-Fi and node, and is processed on the device.
